What is SignHub?

SignHub is a simple web interface for signing and installing iOS apps. Upload an IPA, add your own signing certificate and provisioning profile, and get an install link you can open on your iPhone or iPad.

How it works

  1. Open the Signer and choose your IPA file, or paste a direct IPA URL.
  2. Add your .p12 certificate and its password.
  3. Add the matching .mobileprovision profile.
  4. Optionally change the app's bundle ID, name or version, or add tweaks.
  5. Press Sign IPA, then open the install link in Safari on your device.

What you need

An .ipa file, a valid signing certificate with its password, and a matching provisioning profile. Don't have those? The Certificates page has ready-made options, and the DNS page has DNS profiles.

Signing backend

This site is only the interface. The actual signing is done by an external signing service, so your files and certificate password are sent to it. Only use certificates you are authorized to use, and never share your credentials publicly.

Open source

SignHub is open source. You can read the code, report issues or contribute on GitHub.
