SignHub AntiRevoke DNS

Encrypted DNS profile that blocks Apple's certificate revocation checks.

Install

What is DNS for?

A DNS profile stops your iPhone or iPad from reaching the Apple servers that check whether a signing certificate has been revoked. If the check never goes through, Apple can't tell your device that the certificate is revoked, so your sideloaded apps keep opening.

How it works

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Install on a DNS profile.
  2. Allow the profile to download, then open Settings and tap Profile Downloaded.
  3. Tap Install and confirm with your passcode.
  4. The DNS is now active on your device, and the revocation check is blocked.

Good to know

This reduces the chance of revocation but can't guarantee it. It only helps while the profile is installed, and a revoked certificate can still be flagged when your device is offline or on another DNS. It's meant to be used together with a certificate from the Certificates page.
