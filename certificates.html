What are certificates for?

iOS only runs apps that are signed. A signing certificate is what proves to your iPhone or iPad that an app is allowed to run. Without one, a sideloaded IPA will not install or open.

How it works

  1. Install the certificate pack from this page, or use your own.
  2. Open the Signer and choose your IPA.
  3. Add the .p12 certificate, its password and the matching .mobileprovision profile.
  4. SignHub signs the IPA with the certificate and gives you an install link.
  5. Open the link in Safari to install the signed app.

Good to know

A certificate can be revoked or expire, and then apps signed with it stop opening. Pair it with the DNS profile to reduce the chance of that happening, and only use certificates you are authorized to use.
